Companion apps

Your team works from the bus stop.

Two purpose-built companion apps for your drivers and conductors. Installed as PWAs on any phone — no app store required.

🚕

Driver App

Drivers log in with their staff number and PIN. Complete pre-trip and post-trip vehicle checks. Clock in and out of shifts. Log fuel purchases directly from the bus. All data syncs to the main app in real time.

pmv-driver.3t-ms.com
🏰

Boskru (Conductor) App

Conductors scan RouteKad cashless payment cards and count passengers per trip. Clock in and out of shifts. Submit incident reports directly from the bus. All data appears in the main platform instantly.

pmv-boskru.3t-ms.com
📱
Installs as a real app — no app store required
Both companion apps are Progressive Web Apps (PWA). Staff visit the URL on their phone, tap “Add to home screen”, and the app installs with its own icon. Works on Android and iPhone.

🏭 Tax

Tax Compliance

All employer tax obligations managed in one place. Auto-calculates Salary & Wages Tax (SWT) for every staff member each pay run. Generates compliant tax export files ready to lodge with the tax authority (IRC) online or in person. GST and corporate income tax due dates also tracked.

SWTGSTCITTax ExportIRC
🏛 Super

Superannuation

Automatically calculates employer and employee superannuation contributions each payroll run. Generates the schedule export ready for payment to the superannuation fund (Nasfund). Monthly due dates tracked and alerted so you never miss a contribution.

Employer ContributionEmployee ContributionSchedule ExportNasfund

Road & Transport Compliance

Track every vehicle's road transport registration (RTA), PMV operator permit (CPTA), motor vehicle insurance (MVIL), and all provincial transport authority licences. Expiry alerts ensure no bus operates out of compliance across all 22 provinces.
