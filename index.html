Fleet Management

Register every bus with its plate, make, model and seats. Track the PMV licence, vehicle registration, insurance, safety sticker and roadworthy certificate for each one — whether you renew at MVIL, RTA or CPTA — with reminders before they expire.

Staff & Payroll

RouteKad Payments

Issue RouteKad cashless payment cards to regular passengers. Boskrus scan cards via the Boskru app. Fares post automatically to revenue — no cash handling required.

Cashless · Real-time

Operations

Schedule and manage shifts. Record and investigate incidents. Conduct pre- and post-trip vehicle checks. All logged, timestamped, and retrievable as formal reports.

Incidents · Checks

Timesheets

Drivers clock in and out via the Driver companion app. Boskrus via the Boskru app. Attendance syncs to timesheets automatically. You approve them, and approved hours feed payroll — no paper registers or manual entry.

Real-time · Automated

Revenue & Payments

Track daily revenue by route, by bus, and by payment type. Keep bank and cash accounts, record expenses and other income (bank fees and loans can be switched on if you need them), and see revenue, expenses and profit on your dashboard.

Cash · RouteKad

Compliance Tracking

All PNG Authorities

Reports

Export · Print

Day Close

Switch on Close day in Settings, then one tap closes the day and locks its figures, so numbers can't change by accident. Your day totals are ready for your books or accounting system, and a correction is made on today's date, not by rewriting history.

Locked · Books-ready

Fuel Control

Drivers log fuel from the Driver app, with receipt scanning on higher plans. Every refuel is tied to the right bus and flows into expenses. Bulk fuel depot stock can be switched on if you hold a depot.

Per-bus fuel

Staff ID Cards & Records

Print staff ID cards with a QR code. Keep each driver's licence and PMV endorsement and each boskru's permit on file, with expiry reminders. Choose who in your team can edit and who can only view.

QR · Licences · Roles

GPS & Fuel Level (optional)

Add-on · Live map

🏭 Tax

Tax Compliance

All employer tax obligations managed in one place. Auto-calculates Salary & Wages Tax (SWT) for every staff member each pay run. Generates compliant tax export files ready to lodge with the tax authority (IRC) online or in person. GST and corporate income tax due dates also tracked.

SWTGSTCITTax ExportIRC
🏛 Super

Staff Fund & Superannuation

Staff Fund contributions are calculated each pay run by default. If your business pays into superannuation, switch on Nasfund and 3T PMV calculates employer and employee contributions and prepares the schedule export. Monthly due dates are tracked so you never miss one.
